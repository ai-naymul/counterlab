CounterLab checks the structure of your reasoning. It cannot check your science. Ask a teacher before you run anything.

Your text is sent to Google's Gemini API for analysis. Google is reported to use free-tier inputs and outputs to improve its models, so please don't type your name, your school, or anything personal — this tool doesn't need it. CounterLab itself stores nothing.
